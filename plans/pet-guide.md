# The Big Pet Guide

There are **103 pets** in Unleashed. This guide tells you everything about every one of them.

## How to read this guide

- **Health** is how much damage a pet can take before it faints.
- **Attack** is how hard it hits.
- **Defense** is how tough it is. A high number means it takes less damage.
- These numbers are where a pet **starts**. Every time a pet goes up a level, each number grows by 10% of its starting number.
- **Strength** is how powerful a move is. A bigger number hurts more.
- **Hits up close** moves touch the enemy. **Attacks from far away** moves fly across the screen. **A big hit that might miss** moves hurt a lot, but they can miss.
- **Can use it** tells you how many times a pet can use a move before it needs Pet Food. Weak moves can be used 20 times, medium moves 12 times and strong moves 6 times.
- **Spirits** are the purple gems you collect. Growing a pet up costs 5 spirits.
- **Coins** are what you pay to catch some pets after you beat them in a fight.
- Fire, Ice, Light and Shadow pets live in their own special zones. Now and then (it is very rare) one can also pop up in the older zones.

## Who is strong against who?

Every pet has a **type**. A move does **extra damage** when its type beats the type of the pet it hits. It does **less damage** when the other pet's type beats it.

- **Rock** moves do extra damage to Wood, Fire and Ice pets.
- **Nature** moves do extra damage to Water pets.
- **Wood** moves do extra damage to Nature pets.
- **Water** moves do extra damage to Rock and Fire pets.
- **Normal** moves are never extra strong or extra weak.
- **Light** moves do extra damage to Shadow pets.
- **Shadow** moves do extra damage to Light pets.
- **Fire** moves do extra damage to Ice, Nature and Wood pets.
- **Ice** moves do extra damage to Water, Nature and Wood pets.

Remember: when a type is strong against another, the other type is weak against it. So:

- **Rock** pets take extra damage from Water moves.
- **Nature** pets take extra damage from Wood, Fire and Ice moves.
- **Wood** pets take extra damage from Rock, Fire and Ice moves.
- **Water** pets take extra damage from Nature and Ice moves.
- **Light** pets take extra damage from Shadow moves.
- **Shadow** pets take extra damage from Light moves.
- **Fire** pets take extra damage from Rock and Water moves.
- **Ice** pets take extra damage from Rock and Fire moves.
