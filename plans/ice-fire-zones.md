# Fire and Ice Zones (draft)

This is a first draft. Change anything you like: names, pets, moves, bosses!

---

## The big idea

We are adding two new places to the game:

- **Emberreach**, the Fire zone. It is hot, red and full of lava.
- **Frostmoor**, the Ice zone. It is cold, blue and very slippery.

You can walk to both from the world map. They are past the Shrub Woodlands.

There are also two new pet types: **Fire** and **Ice**.

---

## What is it like to walk around?

- **Lava** (Fire zone): if you step on it, your first pet loses a little bit of health. It never faints your pet. It stops at 1 health.
- **Ice** (Ice zone): you slide when you step on it, like a penguin on a frozen lake!
- **Levels:** the wild pets and bosses match how strong you are. They are never below level 20.

---

## Who beats who?

- **Fire** beats Ice, Nature and Wood.
- **Ice** beats Fire, Nature and Wood.
- Both are weak against Water and Rock.
- Fire and Ice both hurt each other a lot, so a battle between them is exciting!

## Fire pets (Emberreach)

| Pet | How common | What it is like |
|---|---|---|
| **Cinder** | Common | A smouldering coal with a face that hops about. |
| **Kindle** | Common | A fox kit whose tail never stops burning. |
| **Magmite** | Uncommon | A lava-skinned crawler that leaves glowing footprints. |
| **Pyrecrow** | Uncommon | A crow that burns brighter when it is cornered. |
| **Infernal** | Rare | A stag with antlers made of living fire. |

When they grow up, they change shape (this is called evolving):

- Cinder becomes **Ashwing** at level 15.
- Kindle becomes **Blazeling** at level 15.
- Magmite becomes **Calderon** at level 20.
- Pyrecrow becomes **Phoenyx** at level 20.
- Infernal does not evolve. It is already as strong as it gets.

---

## Ice pets (Frostmoor)

| Pet | How common | What it is like |
|---|---|---|
| **Flurry** | Common | A puffball of snow that rolls downhill. |
| **Rime** | Common | A pale owlet with frosty feathers. |
| **Glacet** | Uncommon | A seal-like pet that slides across the ice. |
| **Snowhare** | Uncommon | A fast hare that disappears into the white snow. |
| **Permafrost** | Rare | A mammoth with tusks made of glacier. |

When they grow up:

- Flurry becomes **Blizzard** at level 15.
- Rime becomes **Hoarwing** at level 15.
- Glacet becomes **Glaciarch** at level 20.
- Snowhare becomes **Whitewind** at level 20.
- Permafrost does not evolve.

## New moves

**Fire moves**

- **Ember**: a small spark that burns.
- **Scorch**: a hot blast.
- **Flame Burst**: a big fireball.
- **Magma Fist**: a punch of hot lava.
- **Heat Haze**: a shimmering heat that makes the enemy weaker.
- **Inferno**: the strongest fire move.

**Ice moves**

- **Frost Bite**: a chilly nibble.
- **Chill**: a cold wind that slows the enemy.
- **Ice Shard**: sharp flying ice.
- **Frostburn**: ice so cold that it burns.
- **Blizzard Blast**: a howling snowstorm.
- **Absolute Zero**: the strongest ice move.

**A move for both**

- **Steam Burst**: Fire meets ice, and it makes a huge cloud of steam!

---

## The bosses

Every zone has a boss at the end. If you beat a boss, you get:

- a special move (a TM that you can teach to a pet),
- a rare pet,
- and **half of a key**.

| Boss | Where | Their team |
|---|---|---|
| **Emberlord Vulkar** | Fire zone | Calderon, Phoenyx, Infernal, Magmite |
| **Frostqueen Hailey** | Ice zone | Glaciarch, Whitewind, Permafrost, Rime |

---

## The secret final battle

When you beat **both** bosses, you have both halves of the key. They open a secret arena.

The last boss is the **Twilight Wyrm**. It is a mix of steam and frost. It has 5 pets, and one of them is a brand-new pet called **Steamwyrm**.

---

## Treasure

- **Lures** call more Fire or Ice pets to you.
- **Stones** help your pets evolve.
- **Charms** and **resist items** make your pets stronger or tougher.
- **Chests** hide coins and items.
